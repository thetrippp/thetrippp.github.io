A major puzzle has been identifying the precise mechanism by which particles in these regions are accelerated to such high speeds. The detection of these "transverse" wave motions and fine plumes using high-speed shots from the EUI provides direct empirical evidence for a specific wave-acceleration mechanism, linking energy transfer from the Sun's magnetic fields to the particles of the fast solar wind.

While NASA's MOXIE experiment on the Perseverance rover successfully demonstrated oxygen production from Martian CO2, McKinney's breakthrough likely focuses on scaling up the efficiency and capacity to produce full rocket fuel quantities suitable for a return journey, addressing the comprehensive energy and processing requirements for a complete fuel cycle.
